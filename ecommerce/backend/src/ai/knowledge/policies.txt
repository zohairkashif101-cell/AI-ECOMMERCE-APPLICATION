RETURN POLICY

Customers can request a return within 7 days of receiving the product.

The product must be unused and in its original condition.

Damaged products should be reported to customer support as soon as possible.


REFUND POLICY

Approved refunds are processed after the returned product is inspected.


DELIVERY POLICY

Delivery normally takes 2 to 5 working days.

Delivery times may vary depending on the customer's location.


PAYMENT

Customers can contact support for currently available payment methods.


SUPPORT

Customer support is available Monday to Saturday from 10 AM to 8 PM.
