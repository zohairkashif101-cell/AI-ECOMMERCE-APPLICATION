# Product Requirements Document

## 1. Product summary

The Ecommerce Store is a web application for browsing technology products and
supporting customers after purchase. Customers can create accounts, manage a
cart, read store policies, submit complaints, and ask the support chatbot for
answers grounded in store information. Store owners can manage the catalog and
policies and review and update customer complaints.

## 2. Goals

- Provide a simple, responsive online storefront.
- Keep product and stock information managed by the store owner.
- Let customers track and report support issues.
- Let the owner review complaints and update their status.
- Answer common product and policy questions from store data using retrieval-
  augmented generation (RAG).
- Protect customer and owner actions through authentication and role checks.

## 3. Users

### Visitor

- Browse the landing page and public product catalog.
- Use the chatbot for general product and store-policy questions.
- Register or sign in.
- Must sign in as a customer before submitting a complaint or using a cart.

### Customer

- Sign up, log in, and request a password reset.
- Browse products and maintain a personal cart.
- Submit complaints manually or through the chatbot after reviewing and
  confirming the complaint details.
- View personal complaints and their statuses.
- Read store policies.

### Owner

- Sign in to the owner account.
- Create, update, and delete products.
- Update stock.
- Review customer complaints and change their status.
- Create, update, and delete store policies.

## 4. In-scope functionality

1. **Authentication and authorization**
   - Customer signup and login.
   - Owner signup constrained by configured owner email and invite key.
   - JWT-protected routes and customer/owner role authorization.
   - Email OTP password reset when SMTP is configured.

2. **Catalog**
   - Public product listing and product detail endpoints.
   - Owner-only product creation, update, deletion, and stock management.

3. **Cart**
   - Customer-only cart retrieval and item add/remove operations.
   - Quantities are checked against available product stock.

4. **Policies**
   - Authenticated users can read policies.
   - Owners can create, update, and delete policies.

5. **Complaints**
   - Customers can submit and view their own complaints.
   - Owners can view all complaints and update status.
   - Complaint creation stores an initial customer response and AI-analysis
     fields currently generated by the complaint controller.

6. **Support chatbot**
   - Floating frontend chat widget.
   - Ollama embeddings and generation with product/policy/FAQ context.
   - Complaint intake asks for the product and issue, then asks the customer to
     confirm before using the authenticated complaints API.

## 5. Out of scope

- Payment collection or payment-provider integration.
- Order placement, fulfillment, shipment tracking, or cancellation.
- Guest checkout or anonymous complaint submission.
- Real-time owner push notifications.
- Persistent chat history.
- Vector database or persistent embedding store.
- Production deployment, monitoring, and backup automation.

## 6. Product requirements

| ID | Requirement | Acceptance criteria |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PR-01 | Visitors can browse products. | Product list and detail endpoints do not require authentication. |
| PR-02 | Customers can register and sign in. | Valid credentials produce a JWT and role-bearing user response. |
| PR-03 | Owner access is restricted. | Owner signup must match `OWNER_EMAIL` and `OWNER_SIGNUP_KEY`; owner APIs reject non-owners. |
| PR-04 | Customers can maintain a cart. | Only authenticated customers can read or mutate their cart; quantities cannot exceed stock. |
| PR-05 | Customers can submit complaints. | Complaint is stored as Pending and associated with the authenticated customer. |
| PR-06 | Owners can process complaints. | Owner can list all complaints and set status to Pending, In Progress, or Resolved. |
| PR-07 | The chatbot answers from store knowledge. | Chat API returns an answer and the retrieved source titles, or an explicit error. |
| PR-08 | Chatbot complaint submission requires confirmation. | Widget shows the complaint summary and does not submit until the customer confirms. |
| PR-09 | Password reset is protected. | Reset codes are time-limited, attempt-limited, and require configured SMTP. |

## 7. Success measures

- A customer can complete signup, login, browse, and cart flows without an
  owner-only permission leak.
- An owner can maintain products and policies and process complaints.
- The chatbot can retrieve current MongoDB product and policy facts and return
  sources with its answer.
- Confirmed chatbot complaints appear in the same owner dashboard as
  manually-submitted complaints.

## 8. Assumptions and dependencies

- MongoDB stores users, products, carts, policies, and complaints.
- Ollama is reachable from the backend and has the configured chat and
  embedding models.
- Product prices are displayed in USD in the current storefront.
- Email password reset requires valid SMTP credentials.
- The frontend and backend are run as separate development servers.
